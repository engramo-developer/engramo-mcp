# Connect EngrAmo to your AI assistant

**Other languages:** [Español](../es/README.md) · [Українська](../uk/README.md) · [Русский](../ru/README.md)

Once connected, you can ask your AI assistant in plain words to create flashcard decks, add cards, find
cards or plan your study. Everything it creates lands in your EngrAmo account, ready to study at
<https://study.engramo.app>.

**Before you start:** you need an EngrAmo account. If you don't have one yet, create it at
<https://study.engramo.app>. The sign-in page you'll see during setup has no "sign up" button.

## Pick your app

### In the browser: nothing to install

| App | Guide | Card audio |
|---|---|---|
| Claude (claude.ai, also Claude Desktop and the mobile app) | [Claude](claude.md) | No |
| ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) | [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) | No |
| Gemini (gemini.google.com) | [Gemini](gemini.md) | No |

These take about 2 minutes. You sign in with your EngrAmo email and password; there's nothing to copy
or paste.

### On your computer, with card audio

Want the AI to add spoken audio to your cards (for example, a native-sounding Spanish voice reading each
phrase)? Audio only works with an app installed on your computer, using your own free Google key.

| App | Guide |
|---|---|
| Claude Desktop | [Claude Desktop with audio](claude-desktop-audio.md) |
| Antigravity | [Antigravity with audio](antigravity-audio.md) |

Both need two keys first. [Get your keys](keys.md) shows how (about 5 minutes).

## After setup

Try one of these in a new chat:

```text
Show my EngrAmo catalogs.
```

```text
Create an EngrAmo catalog "Travel Spanish" with 10 cards of useful phrases for a hotel and a
restaurant. Put the Spanish phrase on the front and the English meaning on the back.
```

More ready-to-paste ideas: [prompt examples](../../prompt-examples.md).

## Stop using EngrAmo in an app

Open the EngrAmo app, go to **Settings → Connected apps**, and revoke access for that app. You can also
remove the connection inside the AI app itself (each guide shows where).
